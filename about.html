About Nitin Arvind

Partner, Amber Wealth International · Muscat, Oman

I am a partner at Amber Wealth International, based in Muscat, Oman. I work with investors on transactions and with operators on restructuring, operational improvement, or a path back to profitability.

Eighteen years of that work, mostly in Oman and the wider GCC.

Work

Investors, operators, and the numbers

I have worked with more than $1 billion in investment capital. Turnarounds I led improved profit margins by around 45%. I have closed three M&A transactions with a combined enterprise value of over $15 million.

On the operating side, one receivables reset moved DSO from 78 days to 45 and unlocked more than $500,000 of working capital. A manufacturing change lifted efficiency by about 25%. I have also worked with about five early-stage MENA ventures, in fintech and agritech.

Longer notes sit on the private equity and M&A, working capital, and Oman business pages, and in the blog.
